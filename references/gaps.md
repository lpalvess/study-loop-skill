# Finding a gap

A gap is where the explanation goes vague, skips a step, leans on a word without explaining it, or says something false. Look in this order:

1. **A false claim.** Ask a question whose honest answer exposes it. Do not say "that is wrong" first.
2. **A skipped step.** A cause and an effect with nothing between them ("so it just updates"). Ask what happens in between.
3. **A borrowed word.** A technical term used as if it explained itself. Ask them to say it again without that word.
4. **A missing limit.** They explain when it works but not when it fails. Ask for a case where it breaks.

Ask in their words: "You said X. What happens right before that?" One question, then wait.

If you find no gap, say so, ask for one example they invent themselves, and move on to cards.
