# Writing cards

A card tests one thing. If the answer needs "and", split it.

- Ask for a mechanism ("what happens when...", "why does...") more often than for a definition.
- Use the learner's own phrasing when they gave one.
- Keep the answer to one to three sentences, in the corrected wording.
- No hints inside the question. No yes/no questions.
- One card per gap. Add a card for a new term only if the term will be needed later.
- Make each card stand alone: it must make sense a month from now, without this conversation.
- 3 to 6 cards per session. Drop the weakest first.

Weak: "What is DNS?"
Better: "Your browser has a site name but needs a number to connect. Which step turns one into the other, and who answers it?"
